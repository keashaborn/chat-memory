# Memory V1 V5 initial entity/projection completion gate

Server: seebx backend.

This read-only gate closes the first owner-scoped V5 production-data phase. It
does not stage, review, apply, index, retrieve, or activate memory.

The gate requires:

- the reviewed occupation observation to have applied self and trainer entity
  resolutions, one entity binding, one reviewed projection, and one candidate
  claim;
- the Neko correction observation to remain preserved but deferred because its
  exact pet entity has not yet been grounded in V5;
- no V5 rows for another owner;
- the V5 predicate registry to remain proposed and runtime-inactive; and
- the candidate occupation claim to remain absent from Qdrant.

The deferred correction must not be converted into a new entity from its
anonymous mention. It becomes eligible only after separately grounded evidence
creates or resolves the exact owner-scoped pet entity.

Successful completion opens only the next development boundary: a read-only V5
shadow-retrieval adapter with no prompt or answer-model exposure.
